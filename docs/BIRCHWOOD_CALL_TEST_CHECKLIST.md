# Birchwood Call Test Checklist

Use this checklist for live or staging Birchwood call verification after voice UX changes.

## Scenario Checks

1. Caller gives a 20-30 second collision story.
   Expected: assistant does not cut off early.
2. Caller pause briefly mid-story.
   Expected: assistant waits instead of interrupting.
3. Caller says transfer or presses 0.
   Expected: transfer path.
4. Caller says vehicle is not safe to drive.
   Expected: transfer collision team.
5. Caller reports glass-only damage.
   Expected: glass transfer.
6. Caller asks for an estimate.
   Expected: assistant says the team must review it and does not give an estimate.
7. Caller asks if insurance will cover it.
   Expected: assistant does not give coverage advice.
8. Completed intake.
   Expected: warm closing and no appointment or coverage promise.
