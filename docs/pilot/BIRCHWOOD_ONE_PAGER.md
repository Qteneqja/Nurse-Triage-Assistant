# ORCA × Birchwood Collision — Pilot One-Pager

**What it is.** ORCA answers your collision-intake line, lets the customer
tell the whole story in their own words, asks only the follow-up questions
the story didn't answer, reads the details back for confirmation, and
turns every call into a structured intake record on a simple dashboard —
with a recommended next step for your team.

**What your team does.** Work the dashboard queue: red-flagged records
first, call the customer back, and click the status as you go
(new → contacted → scheduled → completed). That's it — the audit trail
writes itself.

**Safety first.** If a caller mentions anyone was hurt, ORCA immediately
advises seeking medical attention or calling 9-1-1, flags the record in
red, and routes it for your human follow-up before anything else. This
behavior is hard-wired and tested — it cannot be switched off.

**What it is NOT.** ORCA never quotes prices or repair estimates, never
discusses fault or insurance coverage, never books an appointment on its
own, and gives no medical advice beyond the 9-1-1 message. It is decision
support for intake — your people make every decision that matters.

**During the pilot (2–4 weeks).**
- Your dedicated number answers 24/7; long stories are never cut off.
- Every call becomes a dashboard record within seconds of hanging up.
- If anything goes wrong mid-call, the customer hears an apology and a
  promise that your team will call back — and the record is flagged so the
  promise is kept.
- We review the numbers with you weekly: completion rate, call length,
  callback rate, and how the injury flagging performed.

**Honest limits.** Phone speech recognition occasionally mis-hears names
and claim numbers (the read-back confirmation catches most of it, and the
full transcript is on every record). English only for now. Calls are
carried by Twilio and hosted on Microsoft Azure in Canada; no AI language
model processes your customers' calls in this pilot.

**Your contact.** One named operator, reachable through shop hours, with
plain-language updates if anything ever needs your attention.
